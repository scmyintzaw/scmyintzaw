2B Studio explores architecture as a responsive system shaped by environmental forces, urban context, and programmatic complexity, with a focus on the interaction between interior space and external conditions. The project progresses from site-based research in downtown Los Angeles, where environmental and spatial data are mapped and interpreted, into formal experimentation that translates diagrams into spatial prototypes testing relationships such as solid and void or enclosure and exposure. These investigations culminate in the design of a hybrid athletic facility integrated with an urban wind energy system, where atmospheric conditions become both a spatial driver and a resource for energy and ventilation.
