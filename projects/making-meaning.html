Making + Meaning

Course
Making + Meaning
Term
Summer 2025
Instructors
William Virgil, Curime Batliner

Making+Meaning investigates design as a continuous workflow from digital image-making to physical fabrication. It begins with AI-driven explorations of historic architectural forms, using prompting and image generation to produce a library of exaggerated and unconventional figures. These images are translated into collages and sculpted into three-dimensional models, where distortion and recombination become key tools for developing new spatial attitudes. In the second phase, the focus shifts to parametric design. Custom letterforms are generated and developed into complex pattern systems using computational tools. These patterns are then physically produced through robotic stamping onto fabric.
