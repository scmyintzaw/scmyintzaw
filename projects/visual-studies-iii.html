Curate, Capture, Communicate

Course
Visual Studies III · VS4021
Term
Spring 2025
Instructors
Devyn Weiser, Jennifer Chen, Curime Batliner

Visual Studies III frames the architectural portfolio as a designed and authored publication, emphasizing the curation and transformation of drawings, models, images, and texts into a cohesive narrative. It is organized around three modules: Curate, Capture, and Communicate. Each module focuses on a key aspect of portfolio production, including editorial and graphic systems, image-making and representation, and written and visual storytelling across media. The result is a comprehensive portfolio that functions both as a professional document and as a critically constructed body of work.
